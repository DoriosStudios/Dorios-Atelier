## New in 1.1.0 Beta 4 — More Ways to Build

**Dorios’ Atelier 1.1.0 Beta 4 brings new materials, painting tools, and a faster way to repeat your favorite details.** Expand your builds with ornamental stonework, colored concrete and wool, decorative glass, and tools that let you reshape or restyle blocks directly in the world.

### A Larger Building Palette

Explore expanded **Andesite, Basalt, Deepslate, Dripstone, End Stone, and Hardened Netherrack** families, with carved, chiseled, cracked, polished, smooth, brick, tile, and pillar finishes, plus matching slabs, stairs, vertical slabs, and walls. Existing variants are reused, and pillars can be placed along different axes.

The update also adds **22 concrete colors**, each with five building shapes for **110 concrete blocks**, plus matching dyes and **84 decorative glass variants** across colored and uncolored styles. Combine 8 White Concrete with 1 matching dye to craft 8 colored blocks, or start your Nether stonework with the new recipe: **4 Netherrack → 2 Hardened Netherrack**.

The same **22 extra colors now come in wool**, with full blocks, carpets, slabs, stairs, and vertical slabs: **110 wool blocks** grouped in the same color order as the concretes. Craft 8 colored wool with 8 White Wool and 1 matching extra dye, then turn two matching wool blocks into three carpets.

![Expanded Stonework palette][stonework]

Useful for:

- Adding pillars, borders, arches, and textured facades with matching materials.
- Building colored roofs, staircases, paths, and interiors with more shape options.
- Giving windows and skylights a consistent decorative style.

### New Tools for Building in Place

**Furniture Hammers** change supported blocks between full blocks, stairs, slabs, vertical slabs, and walls. Sneak while using a hammer to rotate supported blocks instead of changing their shape. **Glass Cutters** cycle supported glass through its available styles while preserving its color. Both tool families come in **Wooden, Stone, Copper, Iron, Golden, Diamond, and Netherite** tiers.

![Furniture Hammers in all seven material tiers][hammers]

![Glass Cutters in all seven material tiers][cutters]

Use them to:

- Adjust an existing structure without breaking and replacing each block.
- Compare different shapes or glass styles while you build.
- Rotate architectural details into the position you need.
- Carry several material tiers to suit your stage of progression.

### Paint Your Builds

Load a **Paintbrush** or **Paint Roller** by using it in the air with a **Paint Bucket in your offhand**. Brushes recolor one block; rollers cover up to a **3×3 area in the plane of the clicked face**, so painting a wall does not wrap onto the floor. The brush bristles or roller pad show the loaded color. Both tools come in all seven material tiers.

Choose from **38 paint colors**, covering Minecraft’s 16 colors and Atelier’s 22 extras. Each bucket provides **256 successful painting actions**, and a complete roller stroke costs the same single use as one brush stroke. Loading a color and attempting an unchanged or unavailable color are free; depleted buckets become empty paint buckets.

Painting works with matching color-named blocks of **any material**, including compatible blocks from other addons. It preserves supported orientation and shape states. The matching colored variant must exist and follow the recognized naming pattern.

Use painting to:

- Recolor concrete, wool, terracotta, glass, and compatible decorative materials in place.
- Change a wall’s color without rebuilding its stairs, slabs, or other details.
- Repaint larger surfaces with the roller while keeping their original shape.

### Save a Style and Keep Building

**Tool Memory** lets Chisels, Furniture Hammers, and Glass Cutters remember a selected variant. Hold the tool, sneak, and begin mining a supported model block to save its variant; subsequent transformations on compatible blocks follow that selection. Each tool keeps its own saved choice, making repeated patterns and large projects easier to manage.

The **Dorios’ Atelier Settings Book** lets every player choose between **five ways to clear a saved selection**, with options for touch, keyboard and mouse, and controller input. Preferences are saved per player in each world. You receive the book automatically, and replacement copies can be crafted with **1 Book and 1 Stick**. The book and its form support English, Brazilian Portuguese, European Portuguese, Spanish, and Mexican Spanish.

> **Updating an existing world?** Back up your world before installing this beta. It changes Atelier’s block and item identifiers and uses new pack UUIDs; older content is not automatically migrated. Replace the previous Atelier BP/RP pair together and avoid activating both generations in the same world. **Requires Minecraft Bedrock 1.26.40 or newer.**
